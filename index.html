How does self check-in work?

There's a secure lockbox. We send the full address and step-by-step arrival instructions before your stay, so you can arrive any time from 12:00.

Where do the third and fourth guests sleep?

The corner sofa in the living room opens into a comfortable sofa bed with fresh linen. The bedroom has a double bed.

Why book direct rather than on Airbnb?

You deal with your host directly, there's no booking-platform service charge, and we can tailor longer stays. You're always welcome to book on Airbnb too.

Do you offer discounts for longer stays?

Yes: 7% off stays of 7 nights or more, and 28% off stays of 28 nights or more. These are included automatically in the price above.

What is the cancellation policy?

For direct bookings: a full refund if you cancel at least 45 days before check-in. Cancellations less than 45 days before arrival are not refundable. Bookings on Airbnb follow the policy shown there. Because accommodation is booked for specific dates, the statutory 14-day cancellation period does not apply.

Is the Wi-Fi good enough for work?

Yes. Wi-Fi runs at roughly 300–600 Mbps, there's a wired Ethernet connection up to 1 Gbps, and a desk in the bedroom.

Is there parking?

Free street parking is available in the evenings and at weekends. Check the local signs for weekday hours, and ask us before you travel.

How do I know this website is genuine?

This is the only official website for Your London Stay, and our only WhatsApp number is +44 7848 108122. We never ask you to pay by bank transfer, and we never send payment links on WhatsApp. If anything feels wrong, contact us before paying.

Can I arrive early or leave late?

Often, depending on the bookings either side of yours. Send us a message and we'll do our best.
